# Adding a story, news item or diary by hand

The easiest way is the [Share your story form](https://connect-apps.ceh.ac.uk/tech-for-nature-share/), for the team too. To do it by hand on GitHub:

1. Go to `stories/`, `news/` or `diaries/` and click **Add file → Create new file**.
2. Name it `YYYY-MM-DD-a-short-title/index.md`, e.g. `stories/2027-03-14-first-moths-of-spring/index.md`. The folder is created for you.
3. Paste in [templates/post.md](templates/post.md) and fill it in. `description` is the one or two sentences on the card. `image` is the photo on the card.
4. **Commit** it. Then add photos: open the new folder, **Add file → Upload files**. Keep them under about 500 KB (1600 px is plenty), and name them simply, like `photo-1.jpg`.
5. It's live a few minutes later. It appears on the home page and its section page by itself, so there's no menu or list to update.

Not ready yet? Add `draft: true` under the title. Drafts are left off the site until you remove that line.

**Before publishing**, check:
- you have permission to use every photo;
- people who can be recognised are happy to appear;
- no exact locations of sensitive species or private land are given;
- no client or private farm names appear unless agreed.

**Changing or removing a post:** edit or delete its folder.
