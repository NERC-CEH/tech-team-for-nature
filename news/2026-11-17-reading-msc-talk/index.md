On 17 November, students on the University of Reading's MSc in Agricultural Science and Sustainability visited UKCEH. Alyssa Duffy gave a short talk: **"How can automated ID tech support sustainable agriculture research?"**

The talk covered:

- **The devices:** moth light traps such as AMI traps and LepiSense, smaller insect cameras, sound recorders for birds and bats, and phone apps.
- **From photo to data** in four steps: find each insect, sort it, name it, and follow it from photo to photo.
- **Why experts still matter.** AI makes mistakes, so we pair it with expert checking to get records people can trust.
- **What it means on farms:** monitoring insects, birds and bats night after night, across a whole season, on many farms at once.
