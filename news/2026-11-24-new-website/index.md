Our work on automated wildlife monitoring now has one home. This site brings together the old AMBER dashboard and our other project pages: the projects, the technology, how-to guides, and stories from the field.

**What's here:**

- **[Where we work](../../map/index.qmd):** every system we've run, by country. That's **149 moth cameras, sound recorders and bat detectors in 11 countries**, with a few photos from each. We never show exactly where a system is, so each marker sits in the middle of its country.
- **[How-to guides](../../how-to/index.qmd)** with two routes: **do it yourself** with our open-source tools, or **work with UKCEH** and we'll process your data for you.
- **[Projects](../../projects/index.qmd)**, **[stories](../../stories/index.qmd)**, **[news](../index.qmd)** and **[fieldwork diaries](../../diaries/index.qmd)**.
