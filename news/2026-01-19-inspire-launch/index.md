We're starting **Project INSPIRE** (Insect Sensor Platforms for Inclusive Research and Engagement), a three-year partnership between UKCEH and the Aberdeen Group Charitable Trust, which has committed £1 million to tackle the worldwide decline in insects.

INSPIRE builds on [AMBER](../../projects/amber/index.qmd). It begins in the UK, Costa Rica and Singapore, where our systems are already running, and has two parts:

- **Insect sensor platforms:** AI-assisted camera systems that monitor night-flying insects, run with local communities.
- **People as sensors:** citizen science, so that anyone can help record insects.

Read more on the [INSPIRE project page](../../projects/inspire/index.qmd) and in [UKCEH's news story](https://www.ceh.ac.uk/news-and-media/news/partnering-aberdeen-group-charitable-trust-project-inspire).
