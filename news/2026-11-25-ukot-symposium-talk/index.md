On **Tuesday 1 December 2026**, Alyssa Duffy is speaking at the CIEEM UK Overseas Territories Special Interest Group's online symposium, **Technology Enabling Biodiversity Conservation in the UK Overseas Territories**.

Her talk, **"Shining a light on Anguilla's night"**, tells the story of our work with the Anguilla National Trust: carrying automated light traps out to Anguilla's islands, and using AI and expert checking to discover which moths share the island at night.

The symposium runs online from **14:00 to 17:00 GMT**, with talks on how technology is helping conservation across the UK Overseas Territories, including from the Anguilla National Trust.
